MENU CONCEPT

Explore the navigation.

Products, Collections, Offers, Recipes, Services and Discover follow the proposed top-level structure. Offers and Recipes open directly. Services and Discover use submenus; deeper topics sit on their destination previews.

Ranges sit inside relevant product categories and subcategories. Collections bring together themes, occasions and collaborations across categories.

Navigation labels were aligned with the review workbook on 7 November 2026. Range candidates marked for review remain on hold. Memberships and themed product selections still need validation.

The catalogue, NAMI mapping and gap analysis remain in the separate Excel workbook. This prototype contains navigation labels and imagery only, with destination previews.

The interaction follows the observed Lagostina pattern. Contextual range links and parent/subrange destinations are proposals to validate against NAMI’s fixed components.
