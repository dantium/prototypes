Navigation concept · Category and range destinations are previews

MENU CONCEPT

Explore the navigation.

Products, Collections, Offers, Recipes, Services and Discover follow the proposed top-level structure. Offers and Recipes open directly. Services and Discover use submenus; deeper topics sit on their destination previews.

Ranges sit inside relevant product categories and subcategories. Collections are the ranges and editions WMF presents under Collections on wmf.com today: 13 in the menu, with the Fusiontec editions and other collections on the Collections page.

The Menu control in the top bar switches between three structures. “WMF in NAMI structure” shows NAMI categories (level 2) with their subcategories (level 3), as the Lagostina menu does, with NAMI’s own labels; entries WMF would add are tagged New. “NAMI with WMF suggestions” applies the suggestions from the review workbook: Cutlery becomes a category, pressure cookers, sets and removable-handle cookware become subcategories of Pots and Pans, ovenware a subcategory of Bakeware, the small appliance categories are merged into one Kitchen appliances category and the outdoor ones into BBQ & outdoor, the Collections menu holds themes in place of ranges, and labels are tidied. On mobile, this view also opens the menu straight on the product categories, with a search bar and quick links to the other menu sections above the list. “WMF megamenu concept” is the earlier megamenu prototype, with its own header, navigation row and full-width column panels.

Categories, ranges and collections were aligned with the review workbook on 12 November 2026. Each menu shows the eight ranges with the most products; the category page lists them all. Ranges without their own wmf.com page are identified from product titles and still need confirming, as do memberships.

The catalogue, NAMI mapping and gap analysis remain in the separate Excel workbook. This prototype contains navigation labels and imagery only, with destination previews.

The interaction follows the observed Lagostina pattern. Contextual range links and parent/subrange destinations are proposals to validate against NAMI’s fixed components.
